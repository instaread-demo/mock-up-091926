About the Game

Paint and Seek! (Online) is an online multiplayer hide-and-seek game built around camouflage, painting, and object disguise. You can take the role of a Hider or a Seeker. As a Hider, you use the environment to conceal your character. As a Seeker, you inspect the map and search for anything that looks out of place.

🎨 Your Role Changes the Way You Play

Each round gives you one of two main roles.

Hider

Your task is to stay hidden until the round ends. You can use colors and environmental objects to make your character harder to identify.

Seeker

Your task is to locate hidden participants. You need to inspect the surroundings and look for objects or colors that do not match the scene.

The two roles require different approaches. Hiding depends on camouflage. Searching depends on observation.

🖌️ Camouflage Is the Key Mechanic

Paint and Seek! (Online) uses color as part of its hiding system.

You can change your appearance and use colors that match nearby surroundings. Walls, furniture, decorations, and other objects can help you choose a suitable disguise.

The purpose is simple. Your character should be difficult to distinguish from the environment.

🧩 Objects Can Become Part of Your Disguise

The available game documentation also describes an object-transformation mechanic. You can use the environment as part of your disguise. Ordinary objects can provide useful visual cover.

This creates a search system based on observation rather than simple hiding behind a wall or inside a room.

As a Seeker, you need to look for details that do not fit the surrounding area.

👀 Look Closely Before You Make Your Move

Observation is important during the Seeker role.

You can check for:

  • Colors that look different from nearby objects.
  • Shapes that seem unusual.
  • Objects in unexpected positions.
  • Movement in areas that should remain still.
  • Items that do not match the surrounding scenery.

Knowing the map also helps you recognize objects that appear unusual.

🏠 Maps Provide Your Hiding Options

The game uses different maps with environmental objects and possible hiding locations.

The surroundings can include walls, furniture, decorations, and other scene elements. These details matter because they give you places to conceal your character.

As a Hider, you can search for an area with colors that match your appearance.

As a Seeker, you can use your knowledge of the environment to inspect suspicious locations.

⏱️ Survive Until the Round Ends

The Hider has one main objective: remain undiscovered.

The Seeker has the opposite objective. You need to find hidden participants before the round finishes.

The exact round duration can depend on the version or room setup, so a fixed timer should not be treated as a universal setting.

🌐 Online Multiplayer Matches

Paint and Seek! (Online) is designed for online multiplayer sessions.

You can play against other participants in real-time rounds. Current browser listings also describe room-based play, which can support sessions with friends.

The multiplayer format gives both roles a direct purpose. One side searches. The other side tries to remain unnoticed.

🎮 Game Controls

  • W / A / S / D: Move your character.
  • Arrow Keys: Move your character.
  • Right Mouse Button: Look around.
  • Space: Jump.
  • Z: Lock your character.
  • Q: Open the drawing panel.
  • V: Open the pose panel.
  • T: Open the sound panel.

📱 Mobile

  • Use the on-screen movement controls to move.
  • Use the touch interface to look around.
  • Use the displayed jump control when required.
  • Use the available interaction controls for the drawing, pose, and sound functions.
