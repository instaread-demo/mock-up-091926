The Texas Senate race has found an unlikely arena for its sharpest political commentary, with a comedy podcast cutting through where traditional media has hesitated.

Ron White, the scotch-in-hand veteran of the Blue Collar Comedy Tour, sat down with Joe Rogan this week for a conversation that pulled no punches on Republican Senate nominee Ken Paxton.

White, known for his blunt delivery and sardonic wit, did not bother with diplomatic framing when the Texas Attorney General’s name came up during the wide-ranging discussion.

There was no hedging, no “both sides” balancing act, and no carefully worded political qualifications of the kind that typically accompany commentary on sitting officeholders.

“He’s a criminal,” White said. “Just a piece of shit.”

The remarks landed on one of the most listened-to platforms in the world, reaching an audience that traditional political broadcasts have long struggled to capture or hold.

Paxton, who serves as Texas Attorney General, is the Republican nominee in what has become one of the most closely watched Senate races in the country.

His legal and political history has made him a polarising figure, and White’s appearance on Rogan’s show brought renewed attention to questions surrounding his background and conduct.

The moment reflects a broader shift in where consequential political conversations are now taking place, moving away from conventional news formats and into long-form podcast environments.

Joe Rogan’s platform has become a significant venue for political discourse, attracting guests and commentary that shape opinion across a vast and varied listenership in 2026.

The Texas Senate race remains a high-stakes contest, and commentary from figures outside the traditional political press corps is increasingly capable of influencing how voters perceive the candidates involved.

White’s willingness to speak without restraint on a platform of Rogan’s scale ensures the remarks will reverberate well beyond the podcast itself, adding another unpredictable element to an already volatile race.
