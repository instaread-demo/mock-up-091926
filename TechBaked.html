iCloud for Windows: Download, Install & Set Up on PC

If you use an iPhone or iPad but have a Windows PC, you don’t have to rely on a Mac to access your iCloud content. iCloud for Windows is Apple’s official Windows app that brings your iCloud photos, videos, files, passwords, contacts, calendars, and other supported data to your PC.

Once installed, iCloud can integrate directly with Windows. You can access iCloud Drive through File Explorer, view and manage iCloud Photos, use saved iCloud Passwords, and keep supported information synchronized between your Apple devices and PC.

Apple distributes iCloud for Windows through the Microsoft Store, making installation relatively straightforward.

In this guide, we’ll explain how to download iCloud for Windows, install and set it up, access iCloud Photos and Drive, use iCloud without installing the app, and troubleshoot common iCloud problems on a PC.

Download iCloud for Windows

The safest way to download iCloud on a PC is through Apple’s official download page, which directs Windows users to the Microsoft Store.

Official download:
https://support.apple.com/103232

Avoid downloading unofficial iCloud EXE or installer files from third-party websites. They may be outdated, modified, or potentially unsafe.

To download iCloud for Windows:

  1. Open the Microsoft Store on your PC.
  2. Search for iCloud.
  3. Select the official iCloud app published by Apple.
  4. Click Get or Install.
  5. Wait for Windows to download and install the application.
  6. Open iCloud from the Start menu.

You can then sign in and select the iCloud features you want to use on your computer.

iCloud for Windows System Requirements

Before installing the application, make sure your PC meets Apple’s current requirements.

Apple recommends using the latest version of iCloud for Windows with Windows 11 or later. You should also install the latest available Windows updates before setting up iCloud.

Some iCloud features have additional software requirements. For example, integration with Microsoft Outlook may require a supported version of Outlook.

Because Apple periodically updates iCloud and its Windows requirements, check the official Apple support page before installing it on an older computer.

Users searching specifically for iCloud for Windows 10 should note that older iCloud versions may have supported Windows 10, but Apple’s current requirements for the latest release focus on Windows 11 or later.

How to Install iCloud on Windows

After downloading iCloud from the Microsoft Store, setting it up only takes a few minutes.

1. Open iCloud

Open the Start menu, search for iCloud, and launch the application.

2. Sign In to Your Apple Account

Enter the same Apple Account you use with your iPhone, iPad, Mac, or other Apple devices.

Apple previously referred to this as an Apple ID, so you may still see people searching for terms such as “iCloud Apple ID login.”

3. Verify Your Identity

If two-factor authentication is enabled, Apple may send a verification code to one of your trusted devices or phone numbers.

Enter the code when prompted.

4. Choose Your iCloud Features

Depending on your account and software configuration, iCloud for Windows can provide access to features such as:

  • iCloud Drive
  • iCloud Photos
  • iCloud Passwords
  • Contacts and Calendars
  • Browser bookmarks
  • Shared Albums

Turn on the services you want to use.

5. Apply Your Settings

Follow any additional instructions displayed by iCloud and save your settings.

Your selected iCloud information can then begin synchronizing with your Windows PC.

What Can You Do With iCloud for Windows?

Installing iCloud on Windows is particularly useful if you regularly switch between an iPhone and Windows computer.

Instead of manually transferring files and photos between devices, supported content can remain available through iCloud.

iCloud Photos

iCloud Photos for Windows lets you access photos and videos stored in your iCloud Photos library from your PC.

This makes it easier to view photos captured on your iPhone without emailing them to yourself or manually connecting your phone each time.

iCloud Drive

iCloud Drive integrates with File Explorer, allowing you to access files stored in your iCloud account alongside files saved locally on your computer.

iCloud Passwords

Apple provides iCloud Passwords support on Windows, allowing supported passwords stored in iCloud Keychain to be used from your PC.

Browser extensions can also provide password autofill functionality in supported browsers.

Contacts and Calendars

Depending on your software configuration, iCloud can synchronize supported contacts and calendars with Microsoft Outlook.

Shared Albums

You can also work with supported iCloud Shared Albums from Windows, making it easier to access photos shared with friends and family.

How to Access iCloud Photos on Windows

One of the most common reasons people download iCloud for Windows is to access their iPhone photos on a PC.

First, open iCloud for Windows and make sure iCloud Photos is enabled.

After synchronization is configured, you can access iCloud Photos through Windows.

Open:

File Explorer → iCloud Photos

You can then browse photos and videos stored in your iCloud Photos library.

Depending on your configuration and the status of individual files, some items may be stored primarily in iCloud and downloaded to your PC when needed.

This helps reduce the amount of local storage consumed by a large photo library.

How to Download iCloud Photos to Your PC

If you want a photo or video available locally on your computer, open iCloud Photos through File Explorer.

Locate the item you want to download and open or download it as appropriate.

Windows and iCloud may automatically retrieve the full file from iCloud when required.

If you have a large photo library, downloading everything can consume significant storage space. Make sure your PC has enough free disk space before keeping a large number of iCloud photos permanently available offline.

How to Access iCloud Drive on Windows

Once iCloud Drive is enabled, it appears in Windows File Explorer.

Open:

File Explorer → iCloud Drive

From here, you can work with supported files and folders stored in your iCloud Drive.

Files added to iCloud Drive can become available across devices signed into the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive enabled.

For example, you could save a document to iCloud Drive on your Windows computer and later access it from your iPhone, iPad, Mac, or iCloud.com.

How to Keep an iCloud File Downloaded on Your PC

Cloud storage can save disk space by keeping some files online until they’re needed.

However, you may want important files available even when your PC isn’t connected to the internet.

In File Explorer, right-click the appropriate iCloud Drive file or folder and look for the option that keeps the item downloaded or available locally.

The exact wording can vary depending on your iCloud and Windows versions.

Remember that locally downloaded files consume storage space on your PC.

How to Use iCloud Passwords on Windows

Another useful feature is iCloud Passwords.

If you store passwords using Apple’s password system on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac, iCloud for Windows can make supported credentials available on your PC.

Open the iCloud Passwords app after configuring iCloud for Windows.

Apple also provides browser integration for supported browsers, allowing compatible passwords to be filled while browsing websites.

This is especially useful for someone who uses an iPhone with a Windows PC, because it reduces the need to maintain separate password databases on each platform.

For current setup instructions, see Apple’s iCloud Passwords documentation:

https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud-windows/set-up-icloud-passwords-icw2babf5e03/icloud

How to Use iCloud With Microsoft Outlook

iCloud for Windows can also integrate supported contacts and calendars with Microsoft Outlook.

If the feature is available with your configuration:

  1. Open iCloud for Windows.
  2. Sign in to your Apple Account.
  3. Find the option for contacts and calendars.
  4. Enable the feature.
  5. Follow the on-screen setup instructions.
  6. Open Outlook and verify that your iCloud information appears.

Compatibility depends on the versions of Windows, iCloud, and Outlook you’re using.

Apple currently specifies Outlook 2021 or later for the latest supported setup, so users with older versions should check Apple’s current system requirements.

Can You Use iCloud on Windows Without Installing It?

Yes. You don’t necessarily need to download the iCloud app to access your iCloud account from a PC.

Go to:

https://www.icloud.com/

Sign in using your Apple Account.

Depending on your account and enabled iCloud services, the web interface can provide access to services such as:

  • Photos
  • iCloud Drive
  • Mail
  • Notes
  • Contacts
  • Calendar
  • Find My

Using iCloud.com is a convenient option when you’re on another computer or only need occasional access to your iCloud data.

However, the web version doesn’t provide the same Windows integration and automatic synchronization features as the iCloud for Windows app.

iCloud for Windows vs iCloud.com

Feature iCloud for Windows iCloud.com
Installation required Yes No
Access iCloud Photos Yes Yes
Access iCloud Drive Yes Yes
File Explorer integration Yes No
Automatic synchronization Yes No
Windows password integration Supported Limited
Best for Regular PC use Occasional access

If you frequently use iCloud from the same Windows computer, installing iCloud for Windows generally provides better integration.

If you only need to check a file or photo occasionally, iCloud.com may be enough.

Does iCloud Work on Windows 11?

Yes. iCloud for Windows works with Windows 11, and Apple currently recommends Windows 11 or later for the latest version of iCloud for Windows.

You can download the application through the Microsoft Store and then sign in using your Apple Account.

Keeping both Windows and iCloud updated can also help prevent compatibility and synchronization problems.

Can You Download iCloud for Windows 10?

This requires a little more explanation.

Older versions of iCloud for Windows have supported Windows 10, which is why you’ll find many older tutorials and search results describing iCloud for Windows 10.

However, Apple’s current requirements for the latest iCloud for Windows direct users toward Windows 11 or later.

Therefore, you shouldn’t assume that the newest version or every current iCloud feature will work normally on an older Windows 10 computer.

If you’re still using Windows 10, check Apple’s current compatibility information before attempting installation.

You can also use iCloud.com through a supported web browser when installing the current Windows application isn’t possible.

Is There an iCloud EXE File for Windows?

You may find websites advertising an iCloud EXE download, standalone installer, or offline installation package.

For most users, these aren’t necessary.

Apple officially distributes the current iCloud for Windows application through the Microsoft Store.

Using Apple’s official download route helps ensure you’re getting an authentic and current version rather than a modified or outdated installer.

iCloud for Windows Not Working? Try These Fixes

If iCloud won’t open, sign in, download photos, or synchronize files, try the following troubleshooting steps.

Update iCloud

Open the Microsoft Store and check for updates.

Installing the latest available iCloud version can resolve bugs and compatibility problems.

Update Windows

Go to:

Settings → Windows Update → Check for updates

Install available Windows updates and restart your computer.

Restart iCloud

Close iCloud completely and reopen it.

If synchronization has temporarily stopped, restarting the application may resolve the issue.

Restart Your PC

A simple restart can resolve background processes or services that have stopped responding correctly.

Check Your Internet Connection

iCloud depends on an internet connection to synchronize cloud content.

If your connection is unstable, photos and files may remain pending.

Check Your iCloud Storage

If your iCloud storage is full, new photos, videos, backups, and files may not synchronize properly.

You can review your storage usage from your Apple device, iCloud for Windows, or iCloud.com.

Sign Out and Sign Back In

If your account isn’t synchronizing correctly, signing out of iCloud and signing back in can refresh the connection.

Be careful when signing out if you have files that haven’t finished syncing.

Check Apple System Status

Sometimes an iCloud problem isn’t caused by your PC.

Apple maintains a system-status page showing whether services such as iCloud are experiencing an outage:

https://www.apple.com/support/systemstatus/

Reinstall iCloud

If other solutions don’t work, uninstalling and reinstalling iCloud may fix damaged application files or configuration problems.

How to Uninstall iCloud From Windows

If you no longer want iCloud installed on your PC, you can remove it through Windows Settings.

On Windows 11:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select Apps.
  3. Open Installed apps.
  4. Find iCloud.
  5. Click the menu beside it.
  6. Select Uninstall.
  7. Follow the on-screen instructions.

Before uninstalling iCloud, make sure important files have finished syncing.

Removing the Windows application doesn’t delete your Apple Account or automatically erase the data stored in iCloud.

Is iCloud for Windows Free?

Yes. Apple’s iCloud for Windows application is free to download.

However, the amount of cloud storage available depends on your iCloud storage plan.

Apple provides a limited amount of iCloud storage with an Apple Account, while additional storage and other features are available through iCloud+ plans.

Is iCloud for Windows Safe?

Yes, provided you download it through Apple’s official route or the Microsoft Store.

Be cautious with third-party websites offering modified installers, cracked versions, unofficial EXE files, or programs claiming to unlock additional iCloud storage.

You don’t need third-party software simply to access iCloud on a supported Windows PC.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I install iCloud on a Windows PC?

Yes. Apple provides an official iCloud for Windows application that lets compatible Windows PCs access supported iCloud features.

Where can I download iCloud for Windows?

Apple directs Windows users to download iCloud for Windows through the Microsoft Store. Using Apple’s official support page is the safest way to reach the current download.

Can I access iCloud Photos from Windows?

Yes. Enable iCloud Photos in iCloud for Windows, and your supported iCloud photo library can be accessed from your PC.

Can I access iCloud from a PC without downloading anything?

Yes. Visit iCloud.com in a supported browser and sign in with your Apple Account. This is useful if you only need occasional access to your iCloud data.

Does iCloud for Windows work on Windows 10?

Older versions have supported Windows 10, but Apple’s current requirements for the latest iCloud for Windows specify Windows 11 or later. Check Apple’s latest compatibility information if you’re using Windows 10.

Where is iCloud Drive on Windows?

Once iCloud Drive is enabled, you can access it from File Explorer on your PC.

Why are my iCloud Photos not downloading on Windows?

Common causes include an unstable internet connection, insufficient iCloud or PC storage, outdated iCloud software, Windows problems, account authentication issues, or a temporary Apple service outage.

Do I need an iPhone to use iCloud for Windows?

You need an Apple Account and appropriate iCloud services, but the Windows app isn’t limited to people actively using an iPhone. It is designed primarily to give Windows users access to their iCloud content and services.

Conclusion

iCloud for Windows is the easiest official way to bring Apple’s cloud services to a compatible Windows PC. After installing it from the Microsoft Store and signing in with your Apple Account, you can access supported iCloud Photos, iCloud Drive files, passwords, contacts, calendars, and other synchronized data without relying exclusively on an Apple computer.

For regular iPhone-and-PC users, the Windows application offers much better integration than manually transferring files between devices. If you only need occasional access, iCloud.com provides a convenient browser-based alternative without installing software.

For the latest version, system requirements, and download instructions, always use Apple’s official iCloud for Windows page:

https://support.apple.com/103232
