Famous Horse Collar returns to Lambeau Field, joining new menu items

Sponsored by

Among the most indulgent and talked about items on any Lambeau Field menu, the Horse Collar was “retired” in 2016, but 10 years later it’s making a comeback.

USA Today Network via Reuters Connect

September 17, 2026

Reporting by Richard Ryman, Green Bay Press-Gazette / Green Bay Press-Gazette

The Horse Collar is back.

The Horse Collar is a large, horseshoe shaped kielbasa sausage topped with beer cheese sauce, beer mustard and sauerkraut on a horseshoe-shaped Kaiser bun.

It’s one of 15 items added to the Lambeau menu for this season. Others include onion trio burgers, fried brownie bites, Bratzilla grilled cheese, chocolate chip cookie cups served with gelato, fried pickles and more.

“The Horse Collar is a very shareable item,” said Zac Ladubec, Delaware North’s executive chef at Lambeau Field. “It’s that big, fun item we get to talk about.”

Delaware North Sportservice is the concessionaire and food service provider at Lambeau Field.

Advertisement

The Delaware North folks gather at the end of the season to decide what worked, what didn’t and what might be good to add the next season. Testers are readily at hand. “We walk around the building and force people to eat,” Ladubec said.

They also had additional test audiences this year, including two Luke Combs concerts and the Notre Dame/Wisconsin football game, where giant pretzels were a big hit.

Items can be hit and miss – “Nothing here was nailed on the first try,” Ladubec said – and whatever they come up with has to be edible with or without gloves. “The challenge is coming up with menu items that work in August and still work in December,” he said.

“We try to push farther out every year,” said Ladubec, entering his 12th year at Lambeau and sixth year as executive chef.

Here then are the new items at Lambeau and seating sections where they can be bought. Abbreviations mean: I for Inner Concourse; O for outer concourse; P for portable concessions; M for marketplace concessions and U for upper concourse. The first list is concessions areas accessible to fans in the bowl, the premium list is for club seat levels.
