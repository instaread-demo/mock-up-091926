    Will Samsung raise the prices of DRAM and NAND for smartphones?

    Reports from Korean publication Sisa Journal ereveal that Samsung Electronics (the DS division, specifically) wants to increase the prices of DRAM and NAND for smartphones by 7% to 10%.

    However, negotiations with Chinese smartphone makers are already underway. Also, in Q4 (October -December), Apple will be asked back to the negotiating table. This also includes Samsung’s smartphone division. The DS and DX divisions operate separately.

    Apple seems to have a lot of negotiating power as Analysts estimate that the company buys two times as much NAND for its smartphones as Samsung and three times as much as Xiaomi.

    Samsung, as well as SK Hynix and Micron, make huge profits off high-margin memory like HBM and server DRAM. Memory companies centre on high-performance, high-margin memory types instead of the LPDDR that smartphones need.

    Market research firm Omdia shows that the prices of DRAM and NAND increased since last year and will continue to go up. The table below shows the estimated costs of an 8GB/256GB combo and a pricier 12GB/512GB configuration; this is just what the RAM and storage cost.
