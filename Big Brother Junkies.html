Big Brother 28-Tuesday Recap for 9/15/26

Good morning BB Junkies, Yesterday felt almost like a day off for the players. There were a few game conversations but for the most part, it was more of a relaxing and playful day. Usually I get bored when they’re just goofing around but for some reason, yesterday felt more like a reset for them and us. It was like taking a break to gather up the energy for these last couple of weeks.

Although they got an early wake up call, most of them ignored it and stayed in bed longer. I don’t know what the urgency was since the backyard was closed. On top of that, most of them are getting over being sick, currently sick or feeling like they may be getting sick. Hopefully the extra rest did them some good. Here they are toasting with immunity shots to Barrett FINALLY got his clothes back:

I don’t think I’ve mentioned it but Barrett never got his laundry back that was sent off to be cleaned from his HOH. I don’t know if someone forgot about it, temporarily lost it or whatever but he’s got his clothes back now. After spending a couple of days working up a pitch for Dee and Devens, Barrett finally pitched to Devens yesterday.

It was pretty generic and involved being a shield for them and he said he would do whatever they wanted moving forward. He also mentioned how Devens shouldn’t want the guys that stayed in the veto comp for 13 hours to remain in the game. (I don’t think I would have gone that route since he stayed in it about 12 hours and if he loses the blockbuster, he’ll be sitting on the block against either Taylor or Melody) He did at least include how if Devens were on the block against Taylor or Melody, Devens will go home but against Barrett, he might stay.

Let’s just say, if Dee and Devens decided they wanted to keep Barrett, it wouldn’t be because of this pitch. I only mention it because there is a world where Barrett stays even if he doesn’t win the blockbuster. This week could be a tiebreaker vote for Yash but if Barrett were on the block against Taylor and Dee & Devens wanted to keep him, they could. Even if they didn’t have Drew’s vote, they would have Melody’s. (I still don’t see it happening though)

I’m going out of order for the conversations of the day but Yash and Taylor also had a talk about hoping to keep Barrett. Obviously, it wouldn’t be in the scenario above but they did talk about wanting Barrett to stay over Melody. I’m very confused at this point and I don’t understand a lot of Yash and Taylor’s strategy. Is everyone’s goal this season to keep as many comp threats in the game as possible?

Go Ad-Free

Yash and Drew were finally able to check in with each other after they made the deal during the veto comp. This happened a couple of times yesterday but the first time was actually late Monday night after everyone else was in bed. They joked about how everyone thought they were arguing towards the end of the veto comp and how no one realized they were making a deal.

They agreed to keep it going (let everyone continue to think they were arguing) and both said they were serious about “taking it to the end.” From the start, I felt like both of them were lying but Yash might be telling the truth, just a little bit. I don’t think it’s what Yash wants to do but I can kind of see it. He’s made it clear all game he only wanted to work with guys. Believe me, he’s only working with Taylor because of a lack of options. He’d already thought he was aligned with Drew and then felt betrayed when he found out Drew was actually working with the vets. If Yash felt he could trust Drew, I actually think he would like a final 2 with him.

Go Ad-Free

Drew may be a different story. Don’t get me wrong, he could go along with this deal if it kept him safe. However, Drew’s the one person who’s been trying to get everyone to see how big of a threat Yash is for most of the season. If Drew wins the HOH during the double, I just don’t see him keeping Yash safe but you never know. (Drew also agreed to vote Melody out over Taylor this week and I don’t see that one happening either)

Neither of them have told anyone about their deal so thats something. Drew may finally be learning and realized he can keep something to himself. He recently said in a camera talk that if he won, he would nominate Dee and Devens. He might do that but he also might want Yash as his back door target if Yash doesn’t win the veto. I have no idea.

When Taylor and Yash were talking about hoping to keep Barrett, they also had a talk about Drew. Yash tossed out the idea of keeping Drew around a while longer. With more conversation, I don’t know how this one’s going to land with Taylor. She has tunnel vision with wanting to take out to Drew over Devens and Dee.

At least, we got a little more insight into Taylor’s thinking. For some reason, she thinks she’s being made the scapegoat of the season and thinks it’s Drew’s fault. Yash told her she may be the pawn of the season but not a scapegoat. (Of course not because it’s obvious that Drew is the scapegoat of the season! Haha) She said everything keeps getting blamed on her and that makes her the scapegoat.

Dee measuring Taylor and Melody to see which one of them is taller using a cereal box as a level, or a “leveler” if you ask Lala:

I’m not sure what Taylor’s talking about and obviously, she has her own perception of things. I remember once a long time ago, Drew told Kamu that Taylor was crying after someone’s eviction. (I don’t even remember who it was) Kamu told Taylor and then Taylor called Drew out on it. She said she wasn’t crying and to stop spreading rumors about her. That’s the only specific time I can think of that she might be referencing) Yash also mentioned wanting to keep Dee around longer for what thats worth.

That’s pretty much most of the game talk from yesterday. The only other conversation of importance to add is one Devens and Dee had on Monday. While walking in the yard together, they talked about the double eviction. (No, they don’t know but everyone’s assuming it has to be this week) If they can take out Barrett this week, they’re considering trying to take out Yash over Drew during the double. They talked about kind of wishing they could show loyalty to Yash and Taylor since they saved Dee but if it’s a double, it might be too tempting. (It’s also too late in my opinion, there just isn’t time) If Barrett leaves, they know the smarter move is to try to take out Yash. They would end up in a final five with Melody, Taylor and Drew, which they said would be amazing.

Now here’s where they’re playing gets a little wonky for me. They said if Barrett wins the Blockbuster on Thursday, they may have to put up Barrett and Drew together to get Barrett out first. This was on Monday and there’s plenty of time for them to figure out why this may not work. Even if they win the HOH and even if they put those two on the block, I don’t think they’ve counted the votes yet.

I’m not sure how they could take Barrett out over Drew if they were on the Block together. (Maybe if Yash really does want to work with Drew? They can’t depend on that reason because they don’t know about it) I’m assuming they’ll talk again and figure that out before Thursday. They definitely wouldn’t want to put Taylor up against Barrett or Yash but it’s what I think they would need to do to take one of the guys out. (This is assuming Barrett won the Blockbuster and Melody was gone)

I think taking out Yash is the correct move and I don’t think they need to worry about trying to show loyalty because Yash and Taylor saved Dee. (It was dumb and they shouldn’t have done it) Dee told Devens, Taylor kind of gave away their plan when they were watching the veto comp play out in the living room. Taylor had said something about if Barrett won the veto, they could just take him out “when the time came.” I think that basically let Dee know she and Devens were next, right after Drew.

Drew’s put in some good work the past couple of days with Devens and Dee. They may not fully trust him but they’ve at least gone back to saying they love him. Unlike Barrett and Melody, they both said they would vote for Drew in the end. The only debate they had was if they would vote for Drew over Yash. They weren’t sure about that one and it probably doesn’t matter with two more weeks of game to go. I’m just letting you know where they seem to be at for the moment.

I lied, there was one more conversation I wanted to mention from Monday. It was when Melody was talking to Dee. I think she was trying to bond with her a little bit, talking about family and maybe trying to feel her out about the vote. Melody finally got around to telling Dee that she actually wanted to keep the last week and it even talked to Drew about it. Melody said the only reason she didn’t vote to keep Dee was because Taylor and Yash told her the votes weren’t there.

Dee said she was glad Melody told her and said she hadn’t known she was considering it. Ufortunately for Melody, Dee also told her she would need to vote to keep Taylor since Taylor was the one who had just saved her. (I think that was the moment Melody found out she definitely needs to win the Blockbuster)

As I mentioned, Drew’s trying to get the vets to trust him again, even though he doesn’t seem to trust them anymore. He’s also the one who started planting seeds to Devens AGAIN this week about Yash being an unbeatable comp threat. (Maybe they would have talked about it anyway but I actually think Drew is the reason the conversation came up about taking out Yash between Dee and Devens) I honestly have no idea how them liking Drew again will translate into the game. I just know they definitely have a soft spot for him and like him again.

Barrett dancing:

Barrett, unlike Drew, hadn’t done anything since being nominated except complain to the cameras. He’s started to change that because along with pitching to Devens yesterday, he also joined in on some of the chit chat.The gym has become his hiding spot and he’s still spending quite a bit of time in there. He and Dee even shared a hug:

There was a lot of dining room table chat yesterday. They brought up different things that had happened throughout the season and joked about some of the houseguests who’ve already left. They made fun of Drew’s memory wall picture which some of them tried to imitate. This is Melody’s:

When Taylor asked Melody how she could do that, Melody said she learned it in clown school.This was Yash giving it a try:

They also made fun of the haircut Melody gave Drew last Thursday. Everyone knew Melody messed up Drew’s hair but they didn’t realize how badly until Taylor looked at it more closely yesterday:

Taylor said she thought she might be able to fix it but we’ll have to wait and see if she attempts it tomorrow when they get clippers or scissors. (At least Taylor’s hair looked amazing yesterday)

Apparently, whoever was working behind the scenes yesterday was having a bit of fun too. At one point they greyed out Barrett’s memory wall photo. Taylor’s photo was also shrunk and they laughed about the size of Taylor’s head in her picture:

When Barrett got called into the storage room to get his clothing, they all marched in with him. (Yes, Melody and Drew had to practically wrestle along the way):

They cheered for Barrett and his clothing:

Shortly after, they got called back into the storage room because they received their second alcohol delivery of the season:

They also got the camera back:

They took selfies and made videos and basically had a pretty good time with each other yesterday.

Devens and Barrett couldn’t drink the alcohol because they’re have nots but the others seemed to enjoy it.

When Melody was trying to get Drew to use the veto on her, she seemed mad the entire time. Now that it’s over and he used it on himself, they’re back to being normal. They have a very weird dynamic, I’ll just leave it at that.

I know it was kind of a boring day for feed watching. The vibes in the house were good though and I don’t think having that occasionally has to be a bad thing. It’s kind of a moment to hit the pause button and remind themselves that they’re all decent people and they like each other.

I also think we’re kind of fortunate because at least for myself, I can find things I like about everyone left in the house. This is a pretty good group of people to have at the final 7.

Have a great Wednesday.

Mel
