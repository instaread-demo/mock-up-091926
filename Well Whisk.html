Small Business Alarm System Comparison: Features and Providers

Fact Checked
Guide / By Mo

Small business alarm systems split into two camps — DIY self-monitored setups with no contract, and professionally monitored plans that lock you in for years.

A single missed detail can swing your three-year cost by thousands: the monitoring fee you compare today rarely tells you what you’ll owe after install and the contract term. That’s why comparing any small business alarm system means stacking monthly monitoring, equipment, installation, and cancellation terms side by side before you sign.

Below is a straight comparison of the major providers, the features that actually separate them, and the traps that quietly inflate the bill.

DIY vs. Professionally Monitored: What Decides The Fit

The core decision is who watches the system — you, or a monitoring center. DIY and self-monitored systems carry little or no monthly fee but leave you responsible for alerts; professionally monitored systems add round-the-clock coverage and emergency dispatch at a recurring cost.

DIY routes (Ring Alarm, Abode, and camera-first systems like Reolink) typically involve no contract and let you arm or disarm from an app. Professional setups (ADT Business, Vivint Business, SimpliSafe Business) add monitoring center staff, but several require multi-year agreements.

  • Self-monitored: you receive the alerts; no dispatch. Lowest cost, highest responsibility.
  • Professionally monitored: a center watches sensors and contacts emergency services when needed.
  • Hybrid: some providers, like Surety Business, list both tiers so you choose dispatch or not.

Features That Vary More Than You’d Expect

Video verification, cellular backup, and environmental detection are not included across the board — they change by provider and tier. One comparison notes ADT, SimpliSafe, Vivint, and Frontpoint support video verification, while Lorex does not.

Cellular monitoring appears in comparison tables for SimpliSafe, Vivint, Frontpoint, Abode, and Link Interactive. Smoke detection is listed for SimpliSafe, Vivint, Frontpoint, Abode, and Link Interactive, while Vivint, Frontpoint, and Link Interactive also list CO detection.

The caution with Surety Business is specific: one review notes commercial fire alarm monitoring is not included with its security alarm plans, so confirm whether emergency dispatch and environmental alerts are part of the tier you pick.

Access control is a separate line item. Alarm.com’s Access Control runs a $4/month base fee plus $3 per door per month, with bundled packages at 16 doors ($33/mo), 32 doors ($54/mo), and 64 doors ($84/mo) — so count your doors before comparing monthly cost.

Mistakes That Inflate The Real Cost

Equipment price alone hides the true number. Add monitoring, installation, and contract length before judging any quote. A low sticker price with a 36- or 60-month agreement can cost more than a higher monthly fee with no contract.

Watch these four gaps:

  • Assuming camera storage or video verification is included — it varies by provider and tier.
  • Treating CO and fire monitoring as universal — commercial fire monitoring isn’t on every plan.
  • Ignoring cancellation penalties, especially on ADT and Vivint agreements.
  • Mixing consumer and business plans — business tiers have separate apps, user limits, and dashboards.

Federal guidance on information security plans for small organizations, including the NIST SP 800-18 Rev. 2 guide, is worth reading alongside any alarm decision so your physical and data security planning stay aligned.

FAQs

Do business alarm plans require a long-term contract?

It depends on the provider. DIY routes like Ring Alarm and Abode typically require no contract, while professional systems often do — ADT Business uses a 36-month term and Vivint commonly uses 42 or 60 months. Always check the term and early-cancel terms before signing.

Is video verification included on every plan?

No. Video verification varies by provider and tier. One comparison lists it for ADT, SimpliSafe, Vivint, and Frontpoint, but not Lorex. If camera confirmation of an alarm matters to you, confirm the feature is in your specific plan before purchase.

Can I monitor a small business alarm myself?

Yes. Self-monitored and DIY options exist, including free tiers like Abode and camera-first systems such as Reolink that run around $0 per month for monitoring. You’ll receive alerts yourself rather than through a center, and emergency dispatch won’t be automatic.
