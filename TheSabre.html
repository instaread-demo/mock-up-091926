Hoos On Podcast: The Mayo Bowl - Virginia-West Virginia Preview

Preston Willett
September 17, 2026 0
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:05
Instaread

Virginia heads to Charlotte where the mayo flows and West Virginia awaits, while Preston Willett is joined by Ahmad Hawkins from the Virginia Sports Radio Network to preview the Duke's Mayo Classic.

First, Preston and Ahmad discuss the Cavaliers through two games, including the players on each side of the ball surprising people at this point. Then the guys break down the headlines from the matchup between Virginia and West Virginia, including the connections between the two coaching staffs and UVA's opportunity for redemption at Bank of America Stadium.

Later, Preston and Ahmad react to Tony Elliott's press conference, including key injury updates to Jason Hammond and Rico Flores Jr., while discussing the running back, wide receiver, and defensive tackle position groups. Finally, they bring analysis for the matchup with keys to the game, players to watch, and predictions.

You can also follow Preston Willett on Twitter. Make sure to listen here or through Apple Podcasts, Spotify, or your favorite podcast app. Both the Hoos On Podcast and the Preferred Walk-Ons Podcast are part of TheSabre.com Podcast Network.
