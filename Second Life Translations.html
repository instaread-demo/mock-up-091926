        　　Jiujiu pushed himself upright and started getting dressed on his own, then reached out with his little hand to feel Gu Panqiu’s forehead, his face full of worry.

        　　“I’m okay,” Gu Panqiu reassured with a smile. “Early Jiujiu gets breakfast, so what kind of worms do you want today? Daddy’ll make it for you.”

        　　Jiujiu thought about it very seriously. “Then I want two grilled sausages.”

        　　His keyboard was already clattering away early in the morning. Today was clearly going to be busy, but Gu Panqiu was used to seeing him like this. No matter where he was, Fu Yue could settle into work without effort, breaking even the biggest problems down and handling them one step at a time.

        　　Taking a sip of his coffee, Fu Yue gestured toward the breakfast he had made. “I’ll pick Jiujiu up tonight. Just focus on rehearsal.”

        　　Upon hearing that, Jiujiu felt bad for Gu Panqiu and barely touched his food.

        　　Gu Panqiu just smiled. “I’m fine, really.”

        　　“Thanks.” Shen Yao took the cup.

        　　Gu Panqiu’s eyes curved in a smile. “…I’m catching a ride from a friend.”

        　　Yan Zhiyan widened his eyes, feigning surprise. “Wow, lucky you! Mind if I tag along?”

        　　Gu Panqiu hesitated. That isn’t exactly up to him since the car is Fu Yue’s.

        　　Fortunately, Fu Yue came back just then with the medicine. He sat down and glanced at Gu Panqiu. “What’s wrong?”

        　　Gu Panqiu smiled at Yan Zhiyan. “I’m catching a ride with Fu Yue.”

        　　Yan Zhiyan turned to Fu Yue with a polite smile. “CEO Fu, I’m heading to rehearsal with Brother Qiuqiu. Would it be alright if I catch a ride too?”

        Advertisements

        　　With everyone watching, Yan Zhiyan was all but certain Fu Yue would not make him lose face. Shen Yao glanced down at his cup of honey citron tea, a thought crossing his mind. So he finally knows how I felt yesterday.

        　　“No.” Fu Yue finally looked Yan Zhiyan over properly, as if only just registering his face. “There’s a child seat in my car, so there’s no room.”

        　　Shen Yao blinked, stunned. “Since when did you install a child seat?”

        　　“Last week.” Fu Yue set a peeled egg onto Gu Panqiu’s plate. “Gu Panqiu, eat. Even Jiujiu’s faster than you.”

        　　Gu Panqiu shot him a glare, then slowly ate the egg anyway. After taking his cold medicine, he got into Fu Yue’s car and was already dozing off.

        　　“Why’d you turn Yan Zhiyan down just now?” Gu Panqiu questioned, stifling a yawn. “It’s on the way anyway.”

        　　Fu Yue shot him a look, something unreadable flickering in his eyes. “I only drive for my wife.”

        　　Gu Panqiu was already a little out of it, his thoughts going hazy. Another small yawn slipped out, his whole demeanor softening, like a pretty little kitten on the verge of sleep.

        　　A hand smoothed over his soft curls. “Sleep.”

        　　He nodded, eyes closing as he drifted off within moments.

        　　Fu Yue watched him for a moment, then glanced in the rearview mirror at the little one in the back seat.

        　　Out of nowhere, Jiujiu pulled a little snake out of his pocket.

        　　He blinked, then piped up in his soft, babyish voice, “This is Brother Fu Yan’s Sisi. Why is it in my pocket?”

        　　“That kid’s asking for a beating in a pretty creative way.” Fu Yue frowned. “He definitely put it there on purpose.”

        　　Jiujiu did not think the little silver snake was that scary, so he stuffed it into his tiny backpack, planning to give it back to Brother Yanyan at kindergarten later.

        　　“Uncle,” he called out, looking up. “After Daddy wins, he’s gonna work for you, right?”

        　　“That’s right.”

        　　“Then if Daddy gets sick, can you let him rest?” Jiujiu looked at him with wide, hopeful eyes. “He works very hard.”

        　　Fu Yue let out a soft chuckle. “Of course.”

        　　The cold medicine had a sedative effect. By the time Gu Panqiu woke, Jiujiu had already been dropped off at kindergarten.

        　　He blinked awake, then jolted upright, staring at the empty backseat. “Oh my god, good thing you’re here. Otherwise, someone could’ve carried Jiujiu off, and I wouldn’t even have noticed.”

        　　Fu Yue’s gaze softened, a trace of smugness slipping through. That’s because you sleep so soundly when you’re with me.

        　　“Feeling better?”

        　　“A little.” Gu Panqiu sniffed, his voice soft with a hint of grievance. “My head really hurts… my throat too. I’m scared I won’t be able to sing properly.”

        Advertisements

        　　“Then come back early and rest today. I’ll pick you up after Jiujiu finishes school.”

        　　Gu Panqiu nodded. “I’ll see how it goes. You don’t have to worry about me.”

        　　Fu Yue passed him the cold medicine. “Eat properly, and take your meds on time. I’m heading off.”

        　　Now that only ten contestants were left, everyone rehearsed together and took the same vocal classes.

        　　Gu Panqiu was feeling a bit better, but his luck? Still awful, considering he ran into Yan Zhiyan in the elevator again.

        　　“Oh, what a coincidence.” Yan Zhiyan smiled. “I left late, so I thought I’d be way behind you.”

        　　“We dropped Jiujiu off at kindergarten,” Gu Panqiu replied, “and took a bit of a detour.”

        　　Yan Zhiyan nodded. “CEO Fu’s been really good to you, hasn’t he? I heard he confessed last night?”

        　　“Yeah.” Gu Panqiu’s lips curved in a faint smile.

        　　Yan Zhiyan looked at him with wide, innocent eyes. “That must be a lot of pressure, right? Being liked by someone like him. If it were me, I’d feel a bit insecure too.”

        　　Gu Panqiu’s eyes curved slightly. “Oh?”

        　　“You have a kid, after all,” Yan Zhiyan went on, his tone soft with concern. “It’s a complicated situation. Loads of men say they don’t mind, but deep down, they’re still bothered that you’ve had a child with someone else. You should really take a good, hard look before deciding.”

        　　Gu Panqiu found him rather interesting. With a half-smile that did not quite reach his eyes, he nodded. “Yeah, I suppose I should.”

        　　“Why’s your voice so hoarse? That must hurt, huh?” Yan Zhiyan asked. “What are you going to do about vocal class today?”

        　　Gu Panqiu sent Fu Yue a quick text before replying, “It’s fine. It’s a group lesson, so we won’t have to sing much.”

        　　Their vocal coach, Qi Tianle, got started as soon as everyone arrived, launching straight into vocal drills. Yan Zhiyan, however, was distracted, fiddling with his phone every now and then. Seeing how unfocused he was, Qi Tianle frowned. “Yan Zhiyan, come up and give everyone a demonstration.”

        　　Yan Zhiyan froze for a second. “Uh… my throat really hurts, Teacher. I think I’m coming down with something.”

        　　He deliberately lowered his voice until it came out thin and breathy.

        　　Then, almost in a whisper, he added, “Why not let Brother Qiuqiu demonstrate? He took first place last time, didn’t he? He’d definitely do better than me.”

        　　Qi Tianle had no idea what the kid was playing at, but he turned to Gu Panqiu anyway. “Qiuqiu, why don’t you give us a demo?”

        　　Gu Panqiu’s voice really was not in great shape today, but he did not want to make a fuss over it. Simple scales, at least, were still manageable.

        　　“Okay,” he answered. “Give me a second. I’ll get a sip of water.”

        　　Teacher Qi Tianle could tell he was coming down with a cold. Right then, Lan Xiqing’s hand shot up. “Teacher, let me! I’m good at this, hahaha!”

        　　Meanwhile, the one who started it all, Yan Zhiyan, was already lounging by the mirror with a drink. When he noticed Gu Panqiu looking over, he blinked up at him with wide, innocent eyes and even flashed a sweet, guileless smile.

        　　Gu Panqiu’s eyes curved in return. This kid’s not as simple as he looks.
