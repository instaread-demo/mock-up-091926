Vegas’ Early Betting Line For Tennessee Football vs. Texas is Taking Some By Surprise

  • By Ryan Sylvia
  • - Sep 17, 2026

Instaread

Tennessee football has its full attention on this week’s game against Kennesaw State. The Vols will host the Owls on Saturday at 7:45 p.m. ET, with the game airing on SEC Network. However, fans across the country have already circled UT’s next game on the schedule.

On Saturday, September 26, at noon ET on ABC, No. 1-ranked Texas comes to town. The Longhorns are coming off a dramatic come-from-behind win over Ohio State, propelling them to the top-ranked spot in the AP Poll. They have UTSA this week, meaning if things go according to plan, they’ll remain No. 1 when they head to Knoxville.

Tennessee, the No. 15 team in the AP Poll, is naturally an underdog for the game despite being at home. However, the Vols may not be as much of an underdog as you may think. Instead, DraftKings has Texas as just a 4.5-point favorite in the game. This means you can get Tennessee at +150 on the moneyline and the Longhorns at -180.

The over/under point total in the game is set at 56.5 combined points. That means Vegas is expecting the game to finish in the ballpark of 30-26 in favor of Texas, which would be right at the spread and over/under.

So far, on DraftKings, the money is pouring in on the Longhorns, though. The site lists 80% of moneyline bets and all of the bets on the spread to be placed on Texas. That follows the trend of a lot of fans across the country being a bit surprised at how tight the game is projected to be.

Tennessee’s final out-of-conference game before meeting Texas, this weekend’s bout with Kennesaw State, sees the Vols favored by 35.5 points. It will mark the return of former Tennessee running backs coach Jerry Mack as he leads the Owls program in his second year as head coach.
