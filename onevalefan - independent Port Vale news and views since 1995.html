“That shape works” – Jon Brady hints at sticking with wingbacks

Port Vale News Jon Brady 6

11 votes
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:03
Instaread

Port Vale manager Jon Brady says that using wingbacks “works for us a lot more” after his side recorded a 1-0 win over Exeter City.

The Vale manager also suggests that a failure to add wingers in the transfer window (after which head of recruitment Lee Darnborough left his role) was a factor in the decision.

Much as I want to play a back four and attack by playing with wingers, a back three suits this football club at times…

Brady told the media: “I’ve tried a certain way of playing but sometimes the more you look at it, you want to suit the characteristics of what you’ve got.

“In the transfer window we talked about wingers coming in. But, as much as I want to play a back four and attack by playing with wingers, a back three suits this football club at times. I feel that shape works for us a lot more.

“We were a little bit nervy once we went 1-0 up. But this is stuff we can build on.”
